Session 1

Session 1 itinerary

  1. Introductions to the programme

    Meet the course team, understand the shape of the four sessions, and hear what we will be working towards.

  2. Participant survey

    Complete a short starting survey to help us understand confidence, experience, and support needs.

  3. Core lecture and teaching

    What is AI? We will look at AI from past ideas, to everyday tools, to future possibilities.

  4. Break

    A short pause before the hands-on part of the session.

  5. Group work team creation

    Form small groups for the mini challenge work across the programme.

  6. Mini challenge introduction

    Introduce the group challenge and the design sheets that will guide the work.

  7. Personal project: first ideation

    Begin thinking about a personal project idea connected to a real-world need, audience, or community context.

Session 1 Q&A

Do I need to know about AI already?

No. The session starts from the beginning. You can ask questions at any point.

What should I bring?

We will be liaising with you on devices to use in class, you do not have to worry about bringing anything.

What is the mini challenge?

The mini challenge is a group design activity. Your group will use AI and other tools to plan a practical solution to a real-world challenge.

What is the personal project?

The personal project is your own idea.

Does my project need to be finished today?

No. In Session 1, the aim is only to begin. You are choosing a possible direction, not producing a final version.
