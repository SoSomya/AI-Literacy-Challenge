Session 4

Session 4 itinerary

  1. Core lecture and teaching

    We will discuss using generative AI thoughtfully in practice, lessons we have learned and build on the topics we have covered in previous weeks.

  2. Break

    A short pause before the hands-on part of the session.

  3. Mini challenge- short presentations

    In session 4, we will submit final group work onto Padlet (with support in-class). This will allow us to share with the class what everybody had found as they worked through the challenges, ideas they came up with, and the solution recipes they can share.

  4. Personal project - testing, first prototypes & clear outlines

    In this session you will finalise your view of your project. Depending on what you chose to do, this might be putting together final images/posters, an outlined sketch of your complete idea or digital output bringing everything together. This does not have to be completely finalised, it might be an early version, but a good base to build on from.

Summary

The final session brings the course ideas together. In this session teaching will cover any surrounding topics around AI, cover some more depth and present some demonstrations.
