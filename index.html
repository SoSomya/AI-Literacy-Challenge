AI Literacy Challenge Edinburgh 2026

Welcome everyone!

Welcome to the AI Literacy Challenge. This four-session programme will help you explore AI tools, think about safe and ethical use, and design a practical idea that could support your community. Use this website to find session instructions, itinerary for class, and useful links.

  • View the sessions

The 4 Sessions

Session 1: Introduction to AI

Get started with AI in everyday life, learn what the programme is about, and begin recording ideas for your own community project. We will cover what AI is, types of AI and prompting.

  • Open Session 1

Session 2: Ethics and Responsible Use

Explore safe, ethical, and responsible AI use, including privacy, sustainability, trust, and checking outputs.

  • Open Session 2

Session 3: AI for Community Good: Data, models & evaluation

Continue to explore useful tools and shape your practical implementation that could help a group, place, project, or community need.

  • Open Session 3

Session 4: Generative AI in practice

Review the project, improve elements, prepare first snapshots, reflect on what has been learned & share.

  • Open Session 4

Personal Project Build

Build an idea for your community

Across the four sessions, you will develop a practical idea that could help a group, place, project, or community need. This does not need to be a finished product. It can be a plan, prototype, workflow, poster, guide, website idea, or proposal.

Use this section to find:

  • Project tracks and example ideas

  • Questions to help shape your idea

Course team

The AI Literacy Challenge in Edinburgh is led by Edinburgh Futures Institute at the University of Edinburgh. The programme is designed as a supportive, practical space for exploring AI tools, asking questions, and developing ideas for community good.

  • Dr Somya.I — Tutor & curriculum lead
  • Gordon.J — Project Manager: AI Literacy Challenge
  • Sandra.M — Community Engagement Manager
  • Dr Tomas.V — Data Visualisation Engineer

What the team will support with

  • Core teaching blocks to understand the foundations of AI
  • Supporting tool setup and simple troubleshooting
  • Guiding mini-challenge and project work
  • Encouraging safe, ethical, and thoughtful AI use where relevant
