Group work: Solution Design

Mini-Challenge

Mini challenges will be set challenges submitted by community organisations defining a set criteria for you to work through as a group

This part of the class is designed for team collaboration, which means that we will split the the class into roughly 3 groups, with 5 members each. Each group will be given a set challenge to work on. The challenges have been provided by real local community organisations who shared specific tasks that you might work on solving together in your groups using AI as a guiding tool.

During the mini challenge we will provide you with a detailed description of what the task is for your group, some things you might consider and exactly what we are asking you to. We would like you work in yur groups each session and slowly start preparing an outline or plan for how to solve the challenge. In the final session you will submit your process which would look a little like a recipe with instruction to the main task you were dealing with. We will then ask each group to present this to class.

Desiging solutions for real world challenges will allow you explore how to best use AI tools for guiding what might be needed, what the organisation has asked for and how they operate and also some important skills like choosing roles in your team & collecting the results in the document or diagram formats we will provide. Finally, sharing your findings in a presentation and providing feedback to others is a great way to start thinking about what you might prepare, adjust or develop in your own personal projects.
